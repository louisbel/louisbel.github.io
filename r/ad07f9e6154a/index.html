04 · Creative

Ad Studio

Problem

The agency’s first Meta batch took nine weeks from kickoff to a final brief: 18 ads, mostly statics. Team feedback on creative ran through email threads and calls.

What I did

Built an ad studio with Claude Code. It picks photos and clips from the Creative Library on the Mac, writes copy that matches the landing pages, and renders vertical videos in code. The team rates them in a swipe app.

Result

97 ads, 51 of them videos, in two days. The 18 strongest videos went to the team to rate on their phones.

04 · Creative

Competitive intelligence

Problem

Competitor tracking was occasional screenshots. We could not say what rivals were running this week, how they nurture a parent, or what their landing pages promise.

What I did

Built scrapers and agents that collect it on a schedule: ads, nurture emails and landing pages, plus a swipe file. Claude summarizes each run into themes the team can act on.

Result

Rival creative and nurture sequences are now an input to our own: briefs, landing-page redesigns and the nurture rebuild start from them.

05 · Marketing automation

Campaign launcher

Problem

Direct mail lived in a 450-campaign calendar spreadsheet, digital launches in the agency’s plan, and page, email and CRM readiness in people’s heads. Clashes showed up after launch.

What I did

Built one launch board that merges the mail calendar, the digital plan and each program’s build status: landing pages, nurture emails, CRM tests, brochures. It rebuilds whenever the calendar file changes.

Result

18 products and every mail drop on one screen. The most-used tool on the hub: 227 views from 19 people in its first month.

05 · Marketing automation

Weekly update

Problem

Wins, fixes and new tools stayed inside the team that made them. Sales, partners and leadership heard about them late, or not at all.

What I did

Every Friday at 6:30 a Claude agent collects the week’s changes from the workspace and my mailbox and drafts the email in Outlook. I review and edit it, and it goes out first thing Monday morning. Past issues live on the hub, with passwords stripped automatically.

Result

A weekly habit since late September: 6 issues so far, each one under five minutes of my editing.

06 · Reporting

Scorecards

Problem

Reporting meant pulling Power BI exports, HubSpot lists and ad-platform screens by hand. No one could see a program against last year at the same point in its cycle.

What I did

Built four scorecards that refresh themselves every morning from the Azure warehouse, GA4, HubSpot and the ad platforms. Each program lines up with last year from its first mail drop. If a source fails, the page keeps yesterday’s data and says so.

Result

Every program, from mail piece to enrollment, ready before the team logs in. No manual pulls for the weekly review.

07 · System integration

Systems map

Problem

Leads pass through ad platforms, three websites, HubSpot, Dynamics and an Azure warehouse, each owned by a different team. Nobody had the whole picture, so the same lead was counted differently in each report.

What I did

Interviewed stakeholders across marketing, sales and data, with AI drafting the questions and synthesizing the answers. Catalogued the warehouse read-only (3 databases, ~950 tables). Then generated the map as editable Mural widgets through Mural’s MCP.

Result

One shared map: 4 lanes, documented vs inferred flows, 3 open gaps. It is now the brief for moving the stack to Dynamics 365 Customer Insights – Journeys.
