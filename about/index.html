Background
#

I started my career in digital marketing in Berlin, where Groupon and the Rocket Internet ecosystem taught me how to run and test performance marketing campaigns at scale.

I then moved to the US and spent a decade agency-side, helping companies across Retail, B2B SaaS, Banking, EdTech, Higher Ed, and Non-Profit grow their digital presence.

These days I help spread the word on career exploration and summer programs for K12 students and spend the rest of my time exploring what AI can actually do for marketers.

15 years in performance marketing, from a Berlin startup incubator to agency leadership to AI transformation

  2. Head of Media, Creative & Digital – COO Final role

    Managed massive SEM campaigns out of Berlin at a time when optimization meant pulling every lever by hand: bids, match types, account structure, ad copy, and landing pages. Constant experimentation gave me a deep understanding of Google's ad ecosystem and how the auction actually works.
